# ScaleBob Brand Intelligence Kit

An open-source, modular framework for evidence-grounded brand discovery, audit, and operational guidelines.

**Project status:** Specification phase. No installable skills have been released yet.

Planned modules:
1. Brand Audit — existing-company evidence and remediation.
2. Brand Identity Intelligence — discovery and validation.
3. Brand Expression & Guidelines — operational brand rules and assets.

The skills will share a portable, versioned handoff contract. Existing companies can follow Audit → Identity → Guidelines; new companies begin at Identity. Each skill will also support standalone use.

**License:** MIT (to be included in the release). No company-private example assets or proprietary materials will be published.

Development specifications will be reviewed through pull requests before implementation.
